# San Antonio Luxury Real-Estate Event Scout

## Purpose

Identify public San Antonio luxury real-estate events, broker previews, model-home openings, development launches, and property showcases where a premium branded photo activation could support guest experience and marketing.

This skill does not send messages, bypass access controls, or collect private personal information.

## Procedure

1. Ask for target dates, neighborhoods, property type, audience, and activation format.
2. Search public brokerage pages, developer and builder websites, property event pages, chamber announcements, public listing pages, venue calendars, and public social pages visible without login.
3. Prioritize broker previews, luxury open houses, model-home launches, development announcements, agent networking events, and client appreciation events.
4. Verify facts on source pages and record the URL and checked-on date.
5. Capture property or event name, address or public venue, date/time, developer/brokerage, public business contact, audience, sponsors, existing activation, and uncertainties. Never collect private homeowner information.
6. Tag `[HIGH PRIORITY]`, `[LUXURY FIT]`, `[BROKER PREVIEW]`, `[DEVELOPMENT LAUNCH]`, `[CLIENT APPRECIATION FIT]`, `[NEEDS VERIFICATION]`, or `[LOW CONFIDENCE]`.
7. Score +3 public event date, +3 luxury or premium audience, +2 development or launch signal, +2 strong branded guest-experience fit, +1 within 90 days, −2 unavailable business contact, and −2 unverified details.
8. Recommend a premium guest-photo station, broker-preview activation, model-home content moment, or sponsor-branded client event. Do not promise leads, sales, or property exposure.
9. Draft—not send—outreach to a public brokerage, developer, builder, or event contact.

## Output

Return a search summary, full table of leads with source URLs and checked dates, ranked shortlist with evidence and recommended activation, draft outreach, and a checklist covering brand permissions, property access, insurance, staffing, power, privacy consent, and usage rights.

## Safety

Use public business information only. Do not infer private ownership, access gated listings, scrape behind logins, or collect personal buyer/seller data. Never fabricate a property, date, contact, or attendance figure.
