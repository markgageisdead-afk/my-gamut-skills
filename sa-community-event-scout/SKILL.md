# San Antonio Community Event Scout

## Purpose

Identify public community events, HOA gatherings, apartment-community celebrations, neighborhood festivals, pool events, and resident appreciation programs in the San Antonio area that may fit a branded photo booth or recurring resident experience.

This skill does not send messages, bypass access controls, or collect private personal information.

## Procedure

1. Ask for dates, neighborhoods, radius, community type, expected scale, and activation format.
2. Search official HOA or property-management pages, apartment community calendars, neighborhood associations, city calendars, parks pages, public event listings, and public social pages visible without login.
3. Prioritize grand openings, resident events, pool parties, holiday events, family days, amenity launches, and neighborhood festivals.
4. Verify each candidate on the source page and record the URL and checked-on date.
5. Capture event name, date/time, venue, neighborhood, organizer, public business contact, audience, vendors/sponsors, existing activation, and uncertainties.
6. Tag `[HIGH PRIORITY]`, `[RESIDENT EXPERIENCE FIT]`, `[RECURRING FIT]`, `[AMENITY LAUNCH]`, `[FAMILY EVENT FIT]`, `[NEEDS VERIFICATION]`, or `[LOW CONFIDENCE]`.
7. Score +3 recurring or sponsored event, +2 large resident audience, +2 new amenity/community opening, +2 family or premium audience, +1 within 90 days, −2 unavailable organizer, and −2 unverified details.
8. Recommend a resident-appreciation, pool-season, holiday, amenity-launch, or quarterly activation. Do not promise attendance or resident data.
9. Draft—not send—outreach to the property manager, HOA association, venue, or publicly listed event organizer.

## Output

Return a search summary, complete lead table, ranked shortlist with evidence and activation angle, draft outreach, and a verification checklist covering permission, insurance, power, staffing, privacy consent, placement, and terms.

## Safety

Use public business information only. Do not access resident directories, gated groups, private social accounts, or private contact details. Clearly label unknowns and never fabricate event facts.
