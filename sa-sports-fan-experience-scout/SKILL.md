# San Antonio Sports Fan Experience Scout

## Purpose

Find public San Antonio sports events, tailgates, fan festivals, tournaments, athletic fundraisers, and team-community programs that may benefit from a branded photo booth or sponsor activation.

This skill does not send messages, bypass access controls, or collect private personal information.

## Procedure

1. Ask for dates, sport, geography, audience size, activation type, and whether the goal is fan engagement, sponsor visibility, or lead capture.
2. Search official team, league, university, venue, tournament, parks, nonprofit, and public event pages. Use public social pages only when visible without login.
3. Prioritize tailgates, season openers, tournaments, alumni events, fan festivals, charity games, youth sports, and sponsor villages.
4. Confirm event details from source pages and record source URLs and checked-on dates.
5. Capture event, sport, date/time, venue, organizer, public contact, audience, sponsors, vendors, existing photo experience, and uncertainties.
6. Tag `[HIGH PRIORITY]`, `[FAN ENGAGEMENT FIT]`, `[SPONSORSHIP FIT]`, `[TAILGATE FIT]`, `[YOUTH/FAMILY FIT]`, `[NEEDS VERIFICATION]`, or `[LOW CONFIDENCE]`.
7. Score +3 published sponsor/vendor opportunity, +3 strong fan attendance signal, +2 tailgate or premium audience, +2 no activation found in checked sources, +1 within 90 days, −2 unavailable organizer, and −2 unverified date/venue.
8. Recommend sponsor-branded fan photos, team-color templates, a sponsor village station, or a supervised lead-capture activation. Do not promise impressions, attendance, or ROI.
9. Draft—not send—outreach using official organizer and confirmed event details.

## Output

Return a search summary, full lead table, ranked shortlist with evidence, draft outreach for top leads, and a checklist covering rights, permissions, branding approval, insurance, power, staffing, data consent, and commercial terms.

## Safety

Use public sources only. Do not imply team affiliation, use protected marks without approval, scrape behind login, or collect private fan information. Distinguish confirmed facts from inferences.
