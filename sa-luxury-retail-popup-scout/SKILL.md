# San Antonio Luxury Retail Pop-Up Scout

## Purpose

Find San Antonio boutiques, luxury retailers, pop-up brands, and premium storefronts that may benefit from a branded photo activation, product launch experience, or seasonal guest-content station. Focus on public business information in the Pearl, Southtown, downtown, Quarry Market, and other active shopping corridors.

This skill does not send messages, bypass access controls, or collect private personal information.

## Procedure

1. Ask for the target dates, neighborhood, retail format, audience, and preferred activation type.
2. Search official business pages, public event calendars, local lifestyle publications, business-district pages, and public social pages visible without login.
3. Prioritize boutique openings, luxury pop-ups, product launches, seasonal storefront events, new brand arrivals, and neighborhood shopping events.
4. Confirm details from the source page. Record the source URL and checked-on date; do not rely on snippets alone.
5. Capture retailer or brand, business type, location, date, event URL, public business contact, customer type, visual-content signals, existing photo activation, and uncertainties.
6. Tag leads `[HIGH PRIORITY]`, `[POP-UP FIT]`, `[LUXURY BRAND FIT]`, `[FOOT-TRAFFIC FIT]`, `[PARTNERSHIP FIT]`, `[NEEDS VERIFICATION]`, or `[LOW CONFIDENCE]`.
7. Score with +3 premium brand, +3 public launch/pop-up, +2 visual identity, +2 high-traffic corridor, +2 no activation found in checked sources, +1 within 90 days, −2 unavailable contact, and −2 unverified date/location.
8. Recommend a boutique event, product launch, weekend pop-up, seasonal campaign, or recurring placement. Do not promise sales, traffic, or conversions.
9. Draft—not send—short outreach using only confirmed public details.

## Output

Return a search summary, a table of all leads with source URLs and checked dates, a ranked shortlist with evidence and activation model, draft outreach for top leads, and a checklist covering permission, staffing, power, placement, maintenance, usage rights, consent, and commercial terms.

## Safety

Use public information only. Respect site terms, rate limits, and access controls. Do not collect sensitive personal data or contact private individuals through personal accounts. Clearly separate facts, inferences, and unknowns. Guest email or SMS capture must be voluntary and disclosed.
